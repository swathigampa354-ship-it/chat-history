## 3. THE 4 DOMINANT PLAYBOOKS (crowd = ~75%)

1. **Skin Scan → Routine → Track** (~30 projects): selfie → analysis → AM/PM routine → progress. *Us: Glow fits here.* (skinwise, GlowRead, Reflekt, SkinSight, DERMA, PerfectSkinDiary, Face Value, Grapht…)
2. **Apparel VTO + Fit Confidence** (~25 projects): try-on → "is it worth buying?" with confidence/measurement. (FitDNA, FitLens, FitSure, FitTruth, FitCheck, FashionU…)
3. **Color/Palette/Season Analysis** (~15 projects): undertone/seasons → what colors suit you → VTO proof. *Us: Glow's season card fits.* (Undertone ×4, TrueHue, Drape, Drapping, SeeOn, Palette Proof, ToneGrid, Wear Your Best Colors…)
4. **Skin + Style Unified Agent** (~20 projects): one selfie → skin + outfit decided together, often agentic. (MirrorMe, Ensemble, GlowFit, AETHER, MIROIR, Skin2Style, meleon, WearProof…)

> ⚠️ **Our 3 products sit inside playbooks 1–2 — the most crowded.** Standing out requires a §4 differentiator.

| Cluster | Projects | Why it stands out |

## 6. TOP 10 MOST LIKELY WINNERS (my read)

1. **TOLERANCE** — measuring the error bar of beauty scores is brilliant, technical, and honest
2. **MirrorMe / AETHER StyleSense** — unified Skin+VTO+agentic = the exact theme done completely
3. **Aloud** — blind-accessible beauty = maximum impact story
4. **Assay** — skin tracker that says "when it cannot tell" — sophisticated
5. **Dress Circle** — group fitting room is novel + social + demo-friendly
6. **FaceForge** — most memorable/fun; likely "Most Creative"
7. **naxora / LOOK AI** — agentic shopping with real product loops
8. **onpoint** — emerging-market commerce with real payments = huge impact
9. **FitDNA / MirrorProof** — returns-prevention with explainable fit = strong retail value
10. **OneDress** — 6 bridesmaids, provably nobody's worst = great story + measurable

## 7. ACTIONABLE TAKEAWAYS FOR US (before tomorrow's deadline)

**Highest-leverage moves, cheapest to implement:**
1. **Add an "AI-honesty / measurement" feature to Glow** — run the analysis twice (or 3×) on slightly different crops and show the **score range/confidence** ("your pore score: 62 ± 4 — we show you the uncertainty, like TOLERANCE"). ~1–2h work, instantly differentiates Glow from 30 similar skin apps.
2. **Add a "decision receipt" to VirtuFit** — Buy / Compare / Skip verdict after try-on (like FitProof/MirrorProof). ~1h.
3. **Frame the pitch around Impact + honesty**, not features — the crowd is saturated with features; the winners add critical thinking or human impact.
